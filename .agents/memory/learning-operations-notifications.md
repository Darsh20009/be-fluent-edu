---
name: Learning operations and notifications
description: Product rules for Be Fluent's WhatsApp alerts, group placement, and financial reporting.
---

Queue transactional WhatsApp notifications one at a time per account with at least three seconds between sends to reduce account-ban risk. Send administrators important-event summaries with secure app links. Group matching should suggest candidates, but an administrator approves placement. Net profit is based on money actually received minus recurring monthly expenses and teacher compensation per completed lesson; administrators enter monthly costs.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected paced delivery, important-only admin alerts, admin-confirmed groups, and a cash-based profit view with monthly expenses and per-lesson teacher costs.

**How to apply:** Apply these defaults to future notification, enrollment, group-matching, and accounting changes. Keep actual accounting totals derived from recorded transactions and costs.
